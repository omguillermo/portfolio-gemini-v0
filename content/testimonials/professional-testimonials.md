## GoDaddy

### Adelina Green

Senior UX Manager at GoDaddy  
April 17, 2026, Adelina managed Omar directly

I worked with Omar on GoDaddy's Commerce platform, where Omar owned the Catalog, Inventory, Orders and Fulfillment product area as part of delivering a complex multi-year initiative that transitioned both online store and in-person commerce experiences into the new GoDaddy Commerce services. These are some of the most complex areas in commerce, spanning multiple fulfillment types, order states, payment flows, and merchant cohorts across both online and in-person experiences.

Omar took full ownership of this space and operated as the lead designer E2E. Managing a heavy and fast-moving workload across multiple parallel workstreams, Omar ran cross-functional syncs, coordinated handoffs, and kept engineering, product, and content teams aligned. The result was a more cohesive, consistent commerce experience for merchants across both online and in-person channels.

Omar brought a level of care and craft to the work that raised the bar for everyone around. Decisions were grounded in real data and edge cases got accounted for. What defined Omar's approach beyond that was a genuine commitment to getting the experience right for merchants. Omar drove usability testing, brought findings into conversations with product and engineering, and used that research to advocate for the right outcomes.

Any team would be fortunate to have Omar\!
