# Portfolio Expansion Roadmap

This document outlines the strategic expansion of the portfolio, transitioning from a basic showcase into a high-density, system-inspired designer-engineer console.

---

## 1. Web Resume (Career Spec Sheet)
*   **Target Location:** `/about` Page
*   **Goal:** Replace the need for static document downloads with a highly searchable, readable chronological ledger of your experience.
*   **Visual Direction:**
    *   **High-Density Timeline:** Structured like system logs or a git history trail.
    *   **Expandable Role Details:** Clean, monospaced headers detailing `COMPANY`, `ROLE`, `TIMELINE`, and `STACK`. Clicking a role expands to show specific ticket-style accomplishments (e.g. *"[FIX-104] Re-engineered shipping labels page, raising adoption by 18%"*).
    *   **Certs & Education Ledger:** Chronological tables showing certifications (AWS, Scrum, etc.) and education.

---

## 2. Testimonials (System Validation Log)
*   **Target Location:** `/` (Home) or `/about`
*   **Goal:** Provide hard social proof, focusing heavily on validation from cross-functional peers (engineers, PMs, and founders).
*   **Visual Direction:**
    *   **Signed Log Format:** Styled like cryptographic keys or terminal stdout blocks:
        ```text
        [VERIFIED BY: Lead Engineer @ GoDaddy]
        "Omar's Figma files are cleaner than most codebases. He builds what he designs, and understands Git better than some developers."
        ```
    *   **Interactive Tabs:** Easily toggle testimonials by company (`GODADDY`, `QUESTIONPRO`, `STOCKAPP`).

---

## 3. Contact Portal & CTAs (Initiate Session)
*   **Target Location:** Global `/contact` Page or Bottom Section
*   **Goal:** Drive actions from recruiters with a tactile, interactive contact experience rather than a basic mailto link.
*   **Visual Direction:**
    *   **Console Form:** Inputs styled like terminal input lines with custom inline status logs (e.g. `[READY]`, `[VALIDATING...]`, `[TRANSMITTED]`).
    *   **Clear CTAs:** Unified headers and button states throughout the site that read `INITIATE SESSION` or `ESTABLISH CONTACT`.

---

## 4. The Changelog / Notes (Micro-Blog)
*   **Target Location:** `/log` or `/notes`
*   **Goal:** Add a human touch and share design-engineering findings without the pressure of maintaining a traditional "long-form" blog.
*   **Visual Direction:**
    *   **Release Notes Style:** Chronological entries sorted by date (e.g., `LOG_001: Adopting Tailwind 4`, `LOG_002: Dynamic Composer Layouts`).
    *   **Technical Density:** Code syntax highlighting and Figma-to-code workflow diagrams.
